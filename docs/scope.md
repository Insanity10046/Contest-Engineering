# Scope

## Core (owned by the field)

- **Contest architecture modeling** — the five-component model, its couplings, and its failure modes.
- **Adversary decision modeling for design** — how a specific opponent perceives, decides, and adapts, expressed in a form usable for design decisions.
- **Multi-domain sequencing design** — orchestrating moves across physical, informational, legal, and hybrid terrains and phases.
- **Trace-environment design** — treating the evidence record as a designed variable, within lawful limits (see R-4).
- **Design-time evaluation** — metrics and methods for robustness, escalation risk, collateral effect, legitimacy, and cost before deployment.
- **Contest archetype library and benchmark scenarios** — shared, reproducible design challenges.
- **Contest assurance** — auditing a proposed architecture against legal, ethical, and safety constraints prior to use.

## Borrowed (used, not owned)

| Field | What is borrowed | What Contest Engineering adds |
|---|---|---|
| Game theory / mechanism design | Formal languages for incentives and equilibria | Perception, trace, and physical variables; adaptive non-rational actors |
| Decision science | Bias, heuristics, framing, bounded rationality | Application to adversarial design under contest constraints |
| Legal studies / lawfare scholarship | Rule analysis, procedural strategy | Rule systems as designable, testable architectures |
| Forensic science | Trace interpretation, reconstruction, attribution | Trace production as a design variable; forensic readiness |
| Environmental criminology / CPTED | Spatial affordance and behavior channeling | Extension beyond physical space to informational and rule space |
| Military operational design | Sequencing, decision-cycle pressure, multi-domain campaign logic | Generalization to legal, market, and informational contests |
| Intelligence and tradecraft studies | Perception management, secrecy, deception | Engineering constraints, metrics, and assurance |
| Security engineering / systems engineering | Assurance, threat modeling, lifecycle discipline | Adaptive adversaries in open, multi-domain contests |
